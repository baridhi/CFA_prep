# CFA_prep
This repository is meant to house some high level guidance and materials for candidates preparing for the CFA exam by the CFA Institute. I cleared the three levels during 2024 to 2026 in the Portfolio Management pathway. Each of the sub-folders above includes basic guidance and a summary formula sheet with notes for last-minute revision. I am grateful to [Fabian Moa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fabianmoa/) at [Noesis](https://www.linkedin.com/company/noesisklsg/home/) for making his formula sheets publicly available and freely downloadable. I provided an augmented version of those formulae with additional notes and pointers for each of the three levels. Much of the advice/suggestions here is very similar to what you read on [Reddit](https://www.markmeldrum.com/).

The CFA Institute provides detailed historical pass rates and a snapshot of the pass rates in the last 5 years. You can find them [here](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/candidate-resources/exam-results). I am also including the pass rate as of my Charter membership for reference.

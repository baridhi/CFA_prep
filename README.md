# CFA_prep
This repository is meant to house some high level guidance and materials for candidates preparing for the CFA exam by the CFA Institute. I cleared the three levels during 2024 to 2026 in the Portfolio Management pathway. Each of the sub-folders above has includes basic guidance and a summary formula sheet with notes for last-minute revision. I am grateful to [Fabian Moa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fabianmoa/) at (Noesis)[https://www.linkedin.com/company/noesisklsg/home/] for making his formula sheets publicly available and freely downloadable. I provided an augmented version of those formulae with additional notes and pointers for each of the three levels.
